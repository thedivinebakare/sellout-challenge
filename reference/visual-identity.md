# THE DIVINE BAKARE — VISUAL IDENTITY

**Brand:** The Divine Bakare (TDB)
**Positioning:** Sales Funnel Strategist + Web Designer

## Design philosophy

Minimalist + Premium + Strategic.

You prefer designs that feel clean, sophisticated, intentional, spacious, and expensive without being flashy. You don't like clutter, excessive effects, unnecessary decoration, or designs that look overly trendy.

## ✨ Overall Aesthetic

Premium without being flashy.
Minimal without being empty.
Modern without being trendy.
Strategic without looking corporate.

The visual identity should make someone think:
*"This person is intentional, sophisticated, and knows what they're doing."*

And importantly, your preference for minimalist + premium design isn't just a visual preference—it should carry across your websites, social media graphics, presentations, portfolio, proposals, and personal brand.

## TDB visual shorthand

Royal Blue + Pearl White + Strong Typography + Whitespace + Strategic Simplicity.
